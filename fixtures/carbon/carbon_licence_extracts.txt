== https://sandbag.be/carbon-price-viewer/ ==
This Carbon Price Viewer shows the cost of emitting one tonne of Carbon Dioxide in Europe up to April 2026 . 
Up until December 2009, the price shown is based on spot-month continuous contract calculation. After that date, it corresponds to auction settlement prices, from the European Energy Exchange AG. Where does the data come from? Up until December 2009, the price was sourced from Quandl . Since then, the data has been sourced from ICAP .
