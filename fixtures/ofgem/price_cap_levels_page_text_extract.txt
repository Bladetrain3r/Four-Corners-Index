Energy price cap (default tariff) levels Guidance for the energy sector about the maximum price they can charge people on standard variable energy tariff (default tariffs) by region and payment method. If you pay the energy bills in your household We review and update the energy price cap level every 3 months. Read about who the price cap protects and how the energy price cap is calculated in energy price cap and standing charges explained . The actual rates you are charged will depend on where you live, how you pay your bill and the type of meter you have. Get and compare by area energy price cap unit rates and standing charges .
