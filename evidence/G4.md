## 5. Continuity (`checks/explained_moves.md`, generated by `python -m checks.explain_moves`)
Thresholds fixed in METHOD.md section 8 before any value existed: Retail 4%, Wholesale 20% month on month.
- Retail: **0** months above 4% (largest +2.80%, 2023-01).
- Wholesale: **32** months above 20%. Each line separates FX, the local price move, the like-for-like move on the countries present in both months, and country entries, then compares with two held drivers. Result: 0 unexplained by the held drivers; 3 are only PARTLY gas-aligned (2017-01, 2020-06, 2025-03); 9 are attributed to the wind+solar share alone (weaker evidence, labelled as such). The classification thresholds were set after a first look at the moves (disclosed in the file). Bulgaria's entry in 2016-10 is visible as a composition change.
`tests/test_index_outputs.py::test_method_thresholds_match_the_code_and_every_big_move_is_explained` regenerates the file and fails if it drifts, if METHOD.md's thresholds differ from the code's, or if any big move has no line.
