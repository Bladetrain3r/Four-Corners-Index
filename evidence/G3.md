## The open items (all three are real source behaviour, none is a parser fault)
- **B. `ember_yearly_demand_vs_sum_of_monthly`** — Ember's monthly Demand for 2025 does not sum to its yearly Demand: EU -6.06%, CN -1.12%, RU -2.42%, ZA -5.97%; the US matches to 0.000%. Ember's **total generation** differs by the same amounts (EU -6.21%, CN -1.11%, RU -3.67%, ZA -4.81%; US 0.00%), so the two files differ in coverage, not in one definition. Consequence: never combine yearly weights with monthly levels; weights use yearly Demand only.

The committed fixtures do not include that period, so CI is green on it.
