# Sources

*Desk research, 2026-09-29, by a research agent for the conductor; spot-read by the conductor. **Not evidence:** G1 re-verifies every line from this build environment and replaces this file's entries with checked ones. Tags: [T] confirmed by a live keyless request, [P] read from an official page, [S] secondary or search snippet only (unverified).*

## What the research changed in the plan (read first)
- **EU wholesale: Ember instead of ENTSO-E.** Ember publishes EU day-ahead prices as keyless CC-BY CSV (hourly, daily, monthly, from 2015). ENTSO-E needs a token by email request (up to 3 working days) and moved day-ahead to 15-minute resolution on 2025-10-01; keep it as an optional cross-check at G3.
- **Mix for all five regions from one source:** Ember monthly covers China (from 2016-01), Russia (2019-01), South Africa (2018-01), the US and the EU. Ember's download format changed in July 2026; build the parser against the current format only.
- **EU retail runs late:** the latest half-year as of 2026-09-24 is 2025-S2, so plan for provisional months. The EU aggregate is `geo=EU27_2020`. Price components (`_c` datasets) are annual, not semi-annual.
- **HICP nowcast:** the old `prc_hicp_*` datasets froze at 2025-12; use `prc_hicp_minr` (ECOICOP v2, `coicop18=CP0451`).
- **China prices are the weak cell:** there is no national household or industrial average from an official source; provincial agency-purchase prices come as PDF or HTML and cover only part of industrial demand. Proposal for STOP-1: a documented provincial basket of published catalogue tariffs, badge C.
- **Russia:** Rosstat publishes household electricity monthly (XLSX, per 100 kWh, about 11 days' lag); there is no industrial series found; the ATS day-ahead pages were unreachable from the research host (possibly geo-blocked), so the wholesale price may be "no source".
- **Carbon price:** no free, clearly reusable EU ETS series was found (Ember's viewer is Montel-sourced with no download). Expect it to be omitted, and say so on the site.
- **Keys:** only the EIA API key is needed for launch (EIA bulk files are keyless as a fallback). ENTSO-E is optional. Eskom data comes by emailed link, a manual step; Ember covers South Africa's mix monthly.
- **Licences not confirmed:** World Bank Pink Sheet (CC-BY believed, no primary sentence found), Energy Institute (reproduction may need permission), IEA (not freely reusable, so don't use it), CBR (terms not found), EIA hub prices (republished from ICE under permission; check before use). G1 settles each.
- **Nearest existing thing:** the European Commission's energy-prices dashboards (EU against the US, China and Japan; dated to the February 2025 report). Nothing found covers all five regions across retail, industrial, wholesale and mix.

## 1. Eurostat retail prices: nrg_pc_204 (household), nrg_pc_205 (non-household), components

- Codes: `nrg_pc_204` (household, bi-annual), `nrg_pc_205` (non-household, bi-annual), `nrg_pc_204_c` / `nrg_pc_205_c` (price components, ANNUAL not semi-annual) [T]. Also exist: `_h`, `_v` variants (NRG_PC_204_H/_V) [T, dataflow listing].
- Endpoint (JSON-stat, keyless) [T]: `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/nrg_pc_204?geo=DE&currency=EUR&tax=I_TAX&nrg_cons=KWH2500-4999&lastTimePeriod=3&format=JSON`. SDMX 2.1 also answers: `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/dataflow/ESTAT/nrg_pc_204/latest` [T].
- Dimensions [T]: freq=S, siec=E7000, nrg_cons (band), unit=KWH, tax (I_TAX = all taxes and levies), currency (EUR, plus national currency codes), geo, time. NOTE: the band dimension is `nrg_cons`, not `consom` (my first guess returned HTTP 400).
- Bands [T]: household DA `KWH_LT1000`, DB `KWH1000-2499`, DC `KWH2500-4999`, DD `KWH5000-14999`, DE `KWH_GE15000`, plus `TOT_KWH` (all bands). Non-household IA `MWH_LT20`, IB `MWH20-499`, IC `MWH500-1999`, ID `MWH2000-19999`, IE `MWH20000-69999`, IF `MWH70000-149999`, IG `MWH_GE150000`, `TOT_KWH`. The expected "typical" bands (DC and ID) exist and return data. Pre-2007 legacy bands also in the same dataset (different codes).
- Aggregate: use `geo=EU27_2020` (works). `geo=EU` returns an empty set [T]. Sample: EU27_2020 household DC, all taxes, EUR/kWh: 2025-S1 0.2879, 2025-S2 0.2896; DE 2025-S2 0.3869 [T].
- Cadence: half-yearly (S1 = Jan-Jun, S2 = Jul-Dec). Components: annual (latest 2025, dataset updated 2026-08-11/12) [T].
- History start: dataset covers 2007 onwards under the current methodology (dataset title says "from 2007 onwards"); the time axis runs back to 1985-S1 for some countries (legacy bands) [T].
- Lag: as of the 2026-09-24 dataset update the latest period is still 2025-S2 (S1 2026 not yet published) [T]. Exact release calendar not confirmed (tried: dataset metadata, information note link `Info_note_NRG_20260917.pdf` exists in the annotations but I did not open it). Practical lag: roughly 4 months after the half-year ends at best, in this instance about 9 months for S2 2025 to be the newest as of late Sep 2026. Plan for at least 1 semester of staleness.
- Units/currency: EUR (or national currency) per kWh, exclusive/inclusive of taxes by `tax` code (I_TAX seen; X_TAX, X_VAT exist in the standard dataset but I only confirmed I_TAX) [T].
- Key/registration: none [T].
- Licence [P] (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice): "Reuse of statistical data, metadata, publications, and other dissemination tools published on this website for commercial or non-commercial purposes is authorised provided the source is acknowledged." Site editorial content: CC BY 4.0. Caveat on the page: certain data (non-EU countries, some trade data) may not be reused commercially.
- Breaking changes: none found for nrg_pc_204/205 themselves. BUT see item 2 (HICP moved to ECOICOP v2 in 2026).
- Fit: best primary source for EU retail (household DC, non-household ID) - only 2 points a year, so needs a HICP nowcast (below) between releases. Note it also has plain "EU27_2020" so no basket needed inside the EU block, but country weighting is opaque (Eurostat's own weights).

## 2. Eurostat HICP electricity sub-index (monthly nowcast)

- BREAKING CHANGE (surprise): the old datasets `prc_hicp_midx`, `prc_hicp_manr`, `prc_hicp_minr` (COICOP) stopped at 2025-12; their titles now read "(1996-2025)" / "(1997-2025)" and "updated 2026-02-06" [T]. From 2026 data is in the ECOICOP ver. 2 dataset **`prc_hicp_minr`** (title: "Harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP) - ECOICOP ver.2 - indices and rates of change, monthly data"), dimension renamed `coicop18` [T]. The old midx/manr codes will silently return stale 2025-12 data.
- Electricity item: `CP0451` (also `CP04510`), label "Electricity (ND)" [T]. Units in the new dataset: `I25` (2025=100), `I15` (2015=100), `RCH_M`, `RCH_A`, `RCH_MV12MAVR` [T].
- Test [T]: `.../data/prc_hicp_minr?geo=EA&geo=EU&geo=DE&coicop18=CP0451&unit=RCH_A&lastTimePeriod=2&format=JSON` returned 2026-07 and 2026-08 (annual rate: EU 1.6/2.3, EA 2.1/2.8, DE -5.3/-5.6; order of the value array follows geo order EU, EA, DE - check the index mapping before use).
- Cadence monthly; latest month 2026-08 was available by the 2026-09-17 update, i.e. lag about 2.5 weeks [T].
- Key: none. Licence: same Eurostat terms as item 1.
- Fit: good monthly nowcast for the direction of retail price; it is an index of prices paid by consumers (includes all household bands, taxes) so chain it onto the last semi-annual level. Note HICP electricity is not per kWh.

## 3. ENTSO-E Transparency Platform

- Endpoint [P] (Zendesk "Request Endpoint" article, updated 2026-09-21): production `https://web-api.tp.entsoe.eu/api`; IOP/test `https://web-api.tp-iop.entsoe.eu/api`. Https only. Unauthenticated call returns HTTP 401 [T].
- Key/registration: REQUIRED, free. Process [P] (article "How to get security token?", updated 2026-09-28): register at transparency.entsoe.eu, verify email, email transparency@entsoe.eu with subject "RESTful API access", wait up to 3 working days, then "My Account" -> generate token. (Search results mentioned a Keycloak token endpoint `keycloak.tp.entsoe.eu/...` under the "R3" API - this is [S], not confirmed how it interacts with the security-token flow; check the "Restful API" Sitemap article before coding.)
- Rate limit [P] (article updated 2026-09-01): 400 requests/minute per token, not per IP; exceed it and the token may be banned ~10 minutes. Query size limit for Day-Ahead Prices [12.1.D]: one-year date range per request [P].
- Series: Day-ahead prices = article [12.1.D] "Energy Prices" (document type A44), per bidding zone, "Currency/MWh", "published no later than one hour after gate closure" [P]. Actual generation per production type = [16.1.B&C] (document A75) [P: article exists, updated 2026-07-03; I did not read its body].
- Resolution change [P]/[S]: the current schema doc for 12.1.D (r3.1) lists `ResolutionCode`: PT15M, PT30M, PT60M, with an example PT15M [P]. SDAC went live with 15-minute MTU on 2025-10-01 delivery day (go-live 2025-09-30) [S: EPEX SPOT and ENTSO-E SDAC pages appeared in search; not opened]. Consequence: from 2025-10-01 day-ahead series in most bidding zones are 96 points per day (PT15M), not 24; you must average to hourly/daily and handle a mixed-resolution history. Ember's cleaned data says it averages (see item 5).
- Platform migration [P/S]: ENTSO-E launched a "renewed" Transparency Platform (webinar 2025-10-29) and the docs refer to "the transition to the TP R3 API" and "R3.1" schemas, so older documents/client libraries may lag. Not confirmed what the exact cut-over date was.
- Cadence: hourly/15-min day-ahead, daily publication; generation per type up to 15-60 min resolution depending on the country.
- History start: platform data from 2014-2015 (not confirmed on a fetched page).
- Licence [P] (https://transparencyplatform.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/40921911218961-Legal-Terms-and-Conditions, updated 2026-09-22): "Pursuant to clause 2.5 of the Terms of Use, ENTSO-E publishes the list of data which is open for free re-use with no need to seek the prior agreement of the respective Primary Owner of Data." and the list history notes "incorporation of Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY 4.0) on the list of data" (2022-02-18). I did NOT confirm that the specific items (day-ahead prices, generation per type) are on the open-data list; the list itself was not fetched. Not confirmed - check the list before republishing raw series. Earlier terms (2015) were not a licence at all [S].
- Fit: the source for EU wholesale and per-country generation mix, but heavy (per bidding zone, token, 15-min). Ember's monthly/daily price CSV (item 5) is the lighter, already-cleaned alternative and is CC-BY.

## 4. EIA (United States)

- API v2 [P] (https://www.eia.gov/opendata/documentation.php): API key required, free, register at eia.gov/opendata; "EIA data is provided free of charge and should be used in compliance with our Copyrights and Reuse Policy." Unkeyed call returns `API_KEY_MISSING` [T]. Throttling exists, numeric limits not stated on the pages I read ("your API key will be automatically but temporarily suspended").
- Routes: `/v2/electricity/retail-sales` (price by sector/state, monthly), `/v2/electricity/rto` (EIA-930, incl. fuel-type-data; hourly and daily) [P: route names appear in documentation; the fuel-type route detail page returned no content to my fetch tool, so exact facets not confirmed].
- KEYLESS alternative: bulk files, updated per the EIA page "twice daily at 5 a.m. and 3 p.m. Eastern" [P]: `https://www.eia.gov/opendata/bulk/ELEC.zip` (293 MB, updated 2026-09-23), `EBA.zip` (EIA-930 operating data, 693 MB, updated 2026-09-29), `NG.zip` (4.4 MB, 2026-09-24) [T: sizes/dates from HEAD]. I did NOT download them (too large). The bulk manifest is `https://www.eia.gov/opendata/bulk/manifest.txt` [T].
- Retail price: Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.A (cents/kWh, by sector: residential, commercial, industrial, transportation, all). Latest data month July 2026, released 2026-09-24 [P; fetch-tool summary: residential 18.31, commercial 14.53, industrial 9.77, all sectors 14.99 c/kWh - verify against the table before use]. Lag about 8 weeks; the latest month is a "preliminary estimate based on a cutoff model sample" [P]. History: monthly national from 2001 in EPM (start year not re-verified on the page).
- EIA-930 hourly grid monitor: fuel-type generation by balancing authority and lower-48; history start 2015 (July 2015 from my background knowledge; NOT confirmed on a fetched page: the grid monitor "about" page returned only navigation). Lag: the EBA bulk file changed 2026-09-29, so effectively next-day. Caveat: fuel categories are coal/gas/nuclear/hydro/wind/solar/oil/other/storage; EIA-930 data are self-reported and subject to revision (background knowledge, not confirmed here).
- Wholesale: EIA "Wholesale Electricity Market Data" page [P] (https://www.eia.gov/electricity/wholesale/): daily spot prices at 7 hubs (Mass Hub, PJM West, Indiana Hub, ERCOT North, Mid-C, NP-15, SP-15) plus more; "updated biweekly" (per ICE agreement); history from 2001 for Mid-C, PJM West, SP15, Palo Verde, Mass Hub; Indiana Hub 2006; ERCOT North 2014. Format: Excel by year, NO API on that page. LICENCE CAVEAT: data are "republished, with permission, from data collected by the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE)" under an EIA-ICE agreement - not automatically public domain; check before redistributing. Also note US has no single day-ahead price: use PJM West or a load-weighted hub basket, and remember ISO-published DA LMPs (PJM etc.) have their own terms (not researched).
- Henry Hub spot: series `RNGWHHD` (daily, USD per MMBtu), page https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/hist/rngwhhdD.htm shows 1997 to late Sept 2026, "Release Date: 9/23/2026; Next Release Date: 9/30/2026" (weekly release, data to the prior week) [P]. Notable: January 2026 spike above $30/MMBtu (peak listed $30.72) in the series, then back to $2.80-3.00 by late Sept 2026 [P].
- Licence [P] (https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php): "U.S. government publications are in the public domain and are not subject to copyright protection." Ask: acknowledge e.g. "Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration". Some photos/private-source documents are copyrighted. API use also requires acceptance of the API Terms of Service.
- Fit: solid for US retail, industrial, mix, Henry Hub, and consumption. The retail number is a national average by sector (weights inside EIA), which is what the basket needs.

## 5. Ember (ember-energy.org)

- Monthly electricity data page [P]: "88 geographical areas representing more than 90% of global power demand", generation by source, emissions, emissions intensity (gCO2e/kWh), demand; updated twice a month (first week and third week). Global CSV `https://files.ember-energy.org/public-downloads/generation/outputs/release_generation_monthly_global.csv` (28.3 MB, Last-Modified 2026-09-18) [T HEAD]; Europe-only `..._monthly_lower.csv`. Keyless download. Sources: EIA, Eurostat, Energy Institute, national (China NBS) [P].
- Monthly coverage of the target regions [T, via byte-range sampling, not full download]: China (2016-01 to 2026-08), Russia (2019-01 to 2026-07), South Africa (2018-01 to 2026-07), an "EU" aggregate row (present; date range not measured), United States (also present; my sampling window only captured 2001-01 to 2020-02, so US start 2001 is likely but the latest month was NOT confirmed). ASEAN starts 2019-01. Countries such as Germany, Japan, India etc. also present. Note the monthly page's own "Last Updated: 28/07/2026" text is stale relative to the file timestamp (2026-09-18).
- Methodology [S/P]: China monthly thermal, hydro and nuclear come from NBS, thermal disaggregated using IEA monthly generation data (search snippet of Ember methodology; PDF at https://files.ember-energy.org/public-downloads/ember_electricity_data_methodology.pdf not read). Russia and South Africa method: not confirmed.
- BREAKING CHANGE [P]: "Data download format update (July 2026)": now one row per area, month and electricity source, metrics as columns (Generation (TWh), Share of generation (%), Emissions (MtCO2e), Emissions intensity (gCO2e/kWh), YoY columns); "Electricity source" column covers fuels, aggregates, demand and net imports; area names simplified ("United States", "Türkiye"); membership columns are TRUE/FALSE not 1/0; source names are sentence case. Source values seen [T]: Bioenergy, Clean, Coal, Demand, Fossil, Gas, Hydro, Hydro bioenergy and other renewables, Net imports, Nuclear, Other fossil, Other renewables, Renewables, Solar, Total generation, Wind, Wind and solar. Emissions intensity is now per source, overall intensity sits on the "Total generation" row [P]. Any older parser (long format with Category/Variable) will fail.
- Yearly data [P]: 200+ geographies, generation, capacity, emissions, imports and demand; CSV `.../release_generation_yearly_global.csv` (16.1 MB, Last-Modified 2026-09-22) [T]; explorer "Last Updated 15/09/2026". Demand appears as an "Electricity source" = Demand row (TWh) [T]. Same format change as monthly.
- API [P]: `https://api.ember-energy.org/v1/docs`; API key required, free ("No API key set" on unkeyed call [T]); datasets: yearly (200+ geographies), monthly (88 areas), monthly wind and solar capacity (25 countries). Rate limits not stated. The CSV downloads need no key, so use those.
- Licence [P]: "All content is released under a Creative Commons Attribution Licence (CC-BY-4.0)." (stated on the monthly, yearly, API, wholesale price and carbon-price pages).
- European wholesale prices [P/T]: YES. "European Wholesale Electricity Price Data": average hourly (ZIP), daily (CSV), monthly (CSV) day-ahead prices; sourced from ENTSO-e, EMR (UK), SEMOpx (Ireland); missing values interpolated; multi-exchange countries averaged; "Updated monthly" per the page, but the monthly CSV I sampled (119 KB, Last-Modified 2026-09-29) already contains 2026-09 for e.g. Switzerland (167.89 EUR/MWh) and the UK - i.e. a current partial month, so treat the latest month as incomplete [T]. Columns: Country, ISO3 Code, Date, Price (EUR/MWhe); history from 2015-01 [T]. URLs: `https://files.ember-energy.org/public-downloads/price/outputs/european_wholesale_electricity_price_data_{monthly,daily}.csv`, `..._hourly.zip` [T]. This is the recommended free/CC-BY route for EU day-ahead. No US, China, Russia, ZA prices.
- Carbon prices [P]: Ember's "Carbon Price Viewer" / "European electricity prices and costs" tool is updated daily but I found NO download link (no csv/xlsx on the page [T]) and it states the carbon (EU and UK ETS front-December), gas (TTF, NBP) and API2 coal prices are "sourced from Montel" (commercial vendor). The CC-BY line applies to Ember's own content; whether Montel-derived series may be redistributed is NOT confirmed - treat as not reusable. Do not scrape.
- Fit: the anchor source for generation mix and carbon intensity for all five regions plus demand, and for EU wholesale. CC-BY, keyless.

## 6. China

- (a) Tariffs [P/S]. Household: provincial tiered tariffs set by provincial authorities; NO single official national average per kWh found. Third-party figures (Global Petrol Prices, ~CNY 0.532/kWh Dec 2025) are [S] and derived. Industrial/commercial: since Oct 2021 administrative catalogue prices for C&I were cancelled (NDRC documents 1439 and 809, 2021) [S]; users buy from the market or via grid "agency purchase" (代理购电). NDRC General Office notice 发改办价格〔2021〕809号 dated 2021-10-23 [P: NDRC page https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xxgk/zcfb/tz/202110/t20211026_1300892.html appeared in search; summary: prices are calculated monthly and published 3 days before, effective the following month]. Each provincial grid company / DRC publishes a monthly notice (e.g. State Grid Jiangsu PDFs on js.sgcc.com.cn and 95598.cn; Anhui DRC HTML notices) [P: appear in search]. Format: PDF and HTML tables, per province, in Chinese, no API, no aggregator; no consolidated national table found. It covers only the default-supply slice of C&I users, and the price is agency purchase price + network charges + surcharges (a component of the bill, not the whole market price), so it is not a like-for-like "industrial price per kWh". Example values [S]: Jiangsu C&I agency price 0.3937 (Jan 2026), 0.3102 (Feb), 0.3458 (Mar), 0.3715 (Apr) CNY/kWh. Licence: not stated; treat as public notices, unverified for reuse. Accessibility from outside China: State Grid Jiangsu PDFs were returned by search, my own fetches of them not tested.
- Alternative for a national retail/industrial series: IEA Energy Prices (licensed), CEIC (licensed, "36-city electricity price" NDRC price monitoring [S]). Not confirmed any free official national average.
- (b) Generation by type [P]: NBS English "Energy Production in August 2026", published 2026-09-16 (https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202609/t20260916_1965338.html). HTML text with embedded chart images, no data table; gives total generation of industrial enterprises above designated size (943.8 bn kWh, -0.8% y/y for Aug) and y/y rates for thermal (-4.3%), hydro (+2.8%), nuclear (+9.4%), wind (+7.9%), solar (+10.3%) [P; fetch-tool summary]. Absolute TWh by source is in the Chinese-language NBS data query (data.stats.gov.cn), not verified. Note: "above designated size" understates wind/solar (distributed PV) - Ember's China rows correct for this via NBS/other sources [S]. Lag: ~16 days after month end; combined Jan-Feb release. No reuse terms stated on the page ("no explicit copyright or reuse restrictions", per fetch tool).
- (c) Consumption [P]: NEA (nea.gov.cn) monthly "全社会用电量" release: Aug 2026 = 1,033.2 bn kWh (+1.7%), Jan-Aug 7,173.0 bn kWh (+4.3%), residential 1,113.2 bn kWh Jan-Aug, published 2026-09-20 (secondary coverage) and page https://www.nea.gov.cn/20260924/9b291b0f1f524078855c63061fe5ced9/c.html. HTML text, Chinese, ~20 days after month end. CEC: not checked. Reuse terms: not confirmed.
- Wholesale: no national day-ahead market; provincial spot pilots. Not researched further. So (c) wholesale is "n/a" for China unless a province is used.
- Fit: only generation mix and demand are practically obtainable; Ember (item 5) already packages NBS-based China monthly data under CC-BY, use that instead of scraping NBS. Retail/industrial China price is the weakest cell in the whole index.

## 8. South Africa

- Eskom Data Portal [P]: https://www.eskom.co.za/dataportal/ ; supply-side pages (station build-up, hourly renewable generation, hourly demand/available capacity). Bulk download only via an emailed link: "select the required date range... up to a maximum of 5 years" through https://www.eskom.co.za/dataportal/data-request-form/; datasets: demand side, supply side (thermal, nuclear, gas, hydro, pumped storage), pump storage, renewables (wind, PV, CSP), outages. No API mentioned; personal data kept 6 months (POPIA) [P]. No licence/reuse statement found on the pages read (a full Terms of Use page was not located) - "not confirmed". Requires an email address but no credentials; I did not submit the form. Cadence hourly; history: request window is 5 years per request; earliest available date not confirmed.
- Ember alternative: South Africa monthly 2018-01 to 2026-07 CC-BY [T].
- Tariffs [P] (Eskom, 2026-03-16, https://www.eskom.co.za/eskom-implements-nersa-decision-for-financial-year-2027/): Eskom direct customers +8.76% average from 2026-04-01; municipal bulk purchasers +9.01% from 2026-07-01 (different dates because Eskom's financial year runs April-March while municipalities' runs July-June). Corrected upward after the R54.7bn regulatory asset base error: previously 5.36% for direct customers; High Court ordered a redetermination in Dec 2025; NERSA approved 2026-03-05 [P: engineeringnews 2026-03-10]. Residential "Homelight" remains subsidised via an Affordability Subsidy Charge; per-kWh rates were NOT in the notice. The Eskom tariff book/schedule of standard prices PDF (with c/kWh by tariff) is on the page https://www.eskom.co.za/distribution/2026-2027-tariff-increase/ [S: not opened]. Direct vs municipal: most SA households are billed by municipalities, which add their own markups (NERSA-approved separately); an Eskom "average c/kWh" is not the price the typical household pays. NERSA media statement PDF: https://www.nersa.org.za/files/files/2026/03/MediaStatement-NERSAapprovesEskomRetailTariffsandStructuralAdjustmentapplicationfor2026-27.pdf [S].
- Wholesale market [S/P]: NOT operating. SAWEM (South African Wholesale Electricity Market), day-ahead at its core; NERSA granted the NTCSA a market-operator licence in Nov 2025; targets slipped: pv-magazine 2026-07-23 says "originally slated for launch this year", "currently likely to go live in April 2027" [P]. Market Code drafts published (Draft 2.1, July/Aug 2025). So no DA price for ZA.
- Fit: Eskom + NERSA give generation mix, demand and (direct) tariffs; ZA is shown beside the basket, not inside it, so acceptable.

- Licence: NOT confirmed from a primary sentence. I found no licence text inside the xlsx (searched shared strings for licen/CC BY/Creative/copyright: none). Check the dataset's entry in the World Bank Data Catalog before publishing; the underlying gas/coal prices come from commercial sources (Bloomberg etc.).
- Fit: only free monthly series covering TTF, Henry Hub, and two coal benchmarks in one keyless file; daily Henry Hub should come from EIA; there is no free daily TTF here.

## 11. FX

- ECB euro reference rates (keyless) [T]: `https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.USD+CNY+RUB+ZAR.EUR.SP00.A?lastNObservations=1&format=csvdata`. Returns USD, CNY, ZAR for 2026-09-29 (USD 1.1355, CNY 7.6117, ZAR 18.5887 per EUR) and RUB with last observation 2022-03-01 (117.201) [T]. So: USD, CNY, ZAR included; RUB suspended since 2022-03-01, confirmed on the ECB page: it "is not in a position to set a reference rate that is representative of prevailing market conditions" [P]. Published about 16:00 CET on working days, TARGET closing days excluded [P]; daily; history since 1999 (from background knowledge, not re-verified). Same-day rate available same afternoon. Formats CSV/XML/SDMX. "Using the rates for transaction purposes is strongly discouraged" [P].
- Cross rates for USD/CNY, USD/ZAR: derive via EUR (ECB has no direct pair). For RUB use CBR.
- ECB licence [P] (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html): "When such information is distributed or reproduced, it must appear accurately and the ECB must be cited as the source." (summary: custom attribution-based terms; modifications must be stated; the fetch-tool summary called it "non-commercial" - the ECB text quoted does not say that, so commercial reuse is not explicitly confirmed nor excluded).
- Central Bank of Russia [T]: XML `https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_daily.asp` (windows-1251; keyless; 2026-09-30 rates published on 2026-09-29: USD 84.4283, EUR 96.0625, CNY 12.5764, ZAR 51.4274 per 10 ZAR since Nominal=10, RUB per unit). Note the Nominal field (ZAR nominal 10) and decimal comma. History endpoint `https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_dynamic.asp?date_req1=01/09/2026&date_req2=05/09/2026&VAL_NM_RQ=R01235` (USD id R01235) works [T]. English variant `XML_daily_eng.asp` [T]. Rates are for the next business day. CBR gives USD/RUB, EUR/RUB, CNY/RUB, ZAR/RUB directly - so with CBR alone all four target currencies can be converted to USD via RUB, but CBR is official "without assuming any liability to buy or sell" [P].
- CBR licence: not confirmed. The site displays "by using www.cbr.ru you accept the User Agreement" [P]; I could not fetch the User Agreement text (the /eng/about/privacy/ guess returned 404 [T]). Treat reuse as unconfirmed.
- Fit: ECB for EUR/USD/CNY/ZAR daily; CBR only for RUB (the market price of RUB is the CBR official rate; since 2022 RUB has been a distorted, capital-controlled rate, and that matters for interpreting RUB-priced electricity).

## 12. Consumption for basket weights

- Ember yearly [T/P]: "Demand" rows (TWh) for 200+ geographies, CC-BY 4.0 [P], keyless CSV (16.1 MB, updated 2026-09-22) [T]. Free to reuse - recommended. The July 2026 format change applies (item 5).
- Eurostat for the EU [T]: `nrg_cb_e` (annual; `nrg_bal=FC` final consumption, `siec=E7000`, `unit=GWH`) returned 2025 EU27_2020 = 2,412,055 GWh, dataset updated 2026-09-11; `nrg_cb_em` (monthly, 2026-08 available by 2026-09-28) [T]. `nrg_bal=FC_E` returned empty - use `FC`. Licence as item 1.
- EIA (US) and NEA (China) as above; Rosstat/SO UPS for Russia; Eskom for ZA.
- Energy Institute Statistical Review: 2026 edition (75th) released [S: search results, July 2026]; Excel data back to 1965 free to download [S]. Licence NOT confirmed: the EI website returned HTTP 403 to my requests (terms, about, and data downloads pages). A search snippet quotes the report text: "Publishers are welcome to quote from this document provided that they attribute the source ... However, where extensive reproduction of tables and/or charts is planned, permission must first be obtained.", and that "redistribution or reproduction of data whose source is S&P Global is strictly prohibited without prior authorization" [S: not verified against EI page]. One search summary claimed CC-BY-4.0 for the EI review, but the quoted page was Ember's (Ember's own statement), so treat that as wrong/unverified. Conclusion: do not assume EI data is CC-BY.
- IEA [S]: "standalone datasets, data explorers and databases are not made available under a CC BY 4.0 licence as part of the Open Use Terms"; "Electricity Information" is under "Terms of Use for Non-CC Material" (paid product); WEO free dataset is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial) [S: iea.org/terms and product pages, from search snippets]. Not freely reusable in a general dashboard. IEA Energy Prices database (150 countries, industry and household electricity) is also IEA-licensed (paid).
- Recommended: Ember yearly demand as the single weights source (CC-BY, one file, all five regions).
